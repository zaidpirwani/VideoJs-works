Sign-language UX options

Demo for devs, based on a review of 14 sign-language and deaf-focused sites. Clips are real PSL dictionary videos, linked directly from the PSL CDN. Urdu and Roman Urdu titles for colours other than "black", and all caption and transcript text, are sample text.

No reviewed site has a mirror toggle. Handspeak, the ISL dictionaries and the Yale ASL Signbank did not load during the review.

1

Dictionary word page

Upgrade of the current word page. Muted autoplay and loop on. Speed chips 0.25x-1x, saved between words and visits. Replay, frame step, mirror, download. No volume control (word clips have no audio). A badge at the top of the video confirms each change.

  • Speed chips set the speed for every clip on the site (saved in localStorage)
  • Loop is on by default for clips under 10s
  • Mirror: the learner copies the sign as if facing a mirror
  • Keys: , . step one frame, Home start, L loop, R mirror

Pattern from: SignASL (loop, slow, controls below), Spread The Sign (0.25-1 speeds), Auslan Signbank (no volume button), Lifeprint (frame step, keys). Mirror is new.

2

Variant / signer tabs

One word, several signers or regional variants. Tabs swap the clip and keep speed, loop and mirror. This sample uses other colour clips as stand-ins for variants.

Pattern from: SignASL (9 clips per word), Lifeprint (signer switch), SignSchool and The ASL App (multiple signers).

3

Category grid with hover preview

Pointer hover or keyboard focus plays a muted preview at the saved speed. Click loads the word into option 1. With "reduce motion" on, the preview does not play and only the still frame shows.

Pattern from: DCMP (muted previews, honours prefers-reduced-motion), Spread The Sign (silent looping clips).

4

Story / lesson player

Longer videos. Language switch keeps the playback position. Caption tracks (English, Urdu) and a transcript that follows the video and lets you click a line to jump to it. Loop off, speeds up to 2x.

Transcript (sample text)

Pattern from: DCMP (captions, interactive transcript), Lifeprint (back 5s, frame step). Today psl.org.pk serves a separate video file per language with no captions. Captions make each story accessible to hearing learners and screen-reader users (WCAG 1.2.2, 1.2.3).

5

Side-by-side compare

Two similar signs (or two signers, or PSL beside another sign language) played in sync at the same speed. Useful for signs that are easy to confuse.

Pattern from: Spread The Sign (compare checkbox across sign languages).

6

Practice with camera

The sign loops slowly on the left. The learner's own camera shows on the right as a mirror. Nothing is recorded or uploaded. The camera starts only after a click.

Your browser asks for camera permission.

Pattern from: Lingvano (camera practice). Optional, highest effort. Needs HTTPS in production.
